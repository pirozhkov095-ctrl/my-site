Connection guide

Connect to
X179.

Explore the vehicle CAN port, recommended hardware and an example cable setup. Always check the wiring for your specific vehicle before making a connection.

Start here / Vehicle-specific verification

Find your vehicle’s schematic.

The X179 configuration can vary with the model, factory and production date. Confirm the connector view, pin numbers and CAN signals in Tesla’s electrical reference before wiring.

  1. Open the electrical reference for your Model 3 or Model Y.
  2. Select the factory and production date for your vehicle.
  3. In Interactive Schematics, search for X179, CAN Port or PARTY CAN.

02 / X179 example

Know your CAN buses.

The supplied example identifies three vehicle CAN networks for Model 3/Y. Pins below follow the vehicle-side illustration; they are not a substitute for the schematic for your own car.

Chassis CAN / EU Unlock
13 +   14 −

Pin 13: CAN P / High
Pin 14: CAN N / Low

Party CAN / Nag-killer
2 +   3 −

Pin 2: CAN P / High
Pin 3: CAN N / Low

Body / VH CAN / Auto lane change
9 +   10 −

Pin 9: CAN P / High
Pin 10: CAN N / Low

03 / Recommended cable

ENHAUTO GEN2 cable.

Use the following wire groups as a guide to the cable shown. Verify the connector pin numbers and identify CAN High and CAN Low before connecting them to the matching CAN interface on your hardware.

  • Green and white · pins 13–14
    Chassis CAN · EU Unlock
  • Black wires above · pins 2–3
    Party CAN · Nag-killer
  • Yellow and black · pins 9–10
    Body / VH CAN · auto lane change

The colors identify wire groups here; confirm each individual wire’s pin and polarity for your cable.

04 / Power

Power through USB.

USB power from the glovebox is recommended instead of drawing power from the vehicle’s 12 V rail. Check on your vehicle that the USB supply turns off when the car sleeps; behavior may vary.

Connect the selected network’s CAN High and CAN Low to the corresponding CAN interface, after confirming both ends against the appropriate documentation. The interface, firmware and bus selection must match your setup.
